<h3><strong>Still Searching</strong></h3>

<p>
Far from home,<br>
they laugh at the man who keeps searching<br>
for something he cannot name.
</p>

<p>
Let them laugh.<br>
I have no answer to defend,<br>
no destination to announce,<br>
not even a clear reason<br>
for why I am still walking.
</p>

<p>
I have almost nothing.<br>
Not certainty.<br>
Not peace.<br>
Not even the comfort of knowing<br>
what I am afraid of losing.
</p>

<p>
And yet—<br>
I search.
</p>

<p>
For what?<br>
I do not know.<br>
Perhaps that is the only honest answer<br>
I have left.
</p>

<p>
I search through,<br>
through questions that outlive their questioners,<br>
through a small piece of truth<br>
that I barely understand<br>
but cannot leave alone.
</p>

<p>
Perhaps I am not searching for truth.<br>
Perhaps truth is merely the excuse<br>
I give my existence<br>
so that it has somewhere to go.
</p>

<p>
They see nothing in my hands<br>
and call me foolish.<br>
They see no certainty in my eyes<br>
and call me lost.
</p>

<p>
Maybe I am lost.<br>
But what if being lost<br>
is simply the name we give<br>
to a direction that has not yet revealed itself?
</p>

<p>
I have sacrificed enough<br>
to know that a life can be spent<br>
without ever becoming one's own.
</p>

<p>
And sometimes I wonder<br>
whether I am searching for a purpose<br>
or searching for the person<br>
who once believed he had one.
</p>

<p>
There is no great promise waiting for me.<br>
No guarantee that the search will feed me.<br>
No certainty that anyone will remember<br>
what I found, if I find anything at all.
</p>

<p>
Still, I search.
</p>

<p>
Not because I know what is ahead.<br>
Not because I am certain there is something ahead.<br>
But because stopping<br>
would make the emptiness certain.
</p>

<p>
So let them mock me.<br>
Let them name me foolish,<br>
lost, unnecessary, absurd.
</p>


<p>
For perhaps the strangest thing about existence<br>
is not that we find something worth living for,<br>
but that sometimes,<br>
with nothing in our hands<br>
and nothing promised before us,<br>
we still choose to search.
</p>

<p>
I do not know what I am searching for.<br>
I may never know.<br>
</p>

<p>
But I am still searching.
</p>
