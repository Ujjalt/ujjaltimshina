<h3><strong>The Paradox of Being</strong></h3>

<p>
Emptiness is fullness,<br>
and fullness is emptiness.
</p>

<p>
The cup is whole<br>
because it has room for nothing.<br>
The sky is endless<br>
because it holds nothing<br>
that it needs to keep.
</p>

<p>
I emptied myself<br>
to discover what remained,<br>
and found a universe<br>
where I had mistaken silence<br>
for absence.
</p>

<p>
I filled myself with everything,<br>
with names, with dreams,<br>
with the weight of becoming,<br>
until there was no room left<br>
for the one who was becoming.
</p>

<p>
So I let go.
</p>

<p>
And in letting go,<br>
I did not become less.<br>
I became spacious enough<br>
to contain my own contradictions.
</p>

<p>
Perhaps the self<br>
is a strange kind of ocean:<br>
the more it clings to its waters,<br>
the smaller it seems;<br>
the more it yields to the horizon,<br>
the less its boundaries mean.
</p>

<p>
To be empty<br>
is not necessarily to lack.<br>
To be full<br>
is not necessarily to possess.
</p>

<p>
Sometimes, the emptiest heart<br>
has room for the whole world,<br>
while the fullest one<br>
cannot accommodate a single breath.
</p>

<p>
And somewhere between<br>
the nothing I fear<br>
and the everything I seek,<br>
I meet myself.
</p>

<p>
Not as something to be filled,<br>
not as something to be emptied,<br>
but as the quiet space<br>
in which both arise<br>
and disappear.
</p>

<p>
I spend my life gathering pieces<br>
of everything I wish to be,<br>
building a self out of fragments,<br>
hoping that one day<br>
the pieces will make me whole.
</p>

<p>
Yet the more I gather,<br>
the more I discover<br>
how much I can live without.
</p>

<p>
Perhaps I become everything<br>
when I stop demanding to be something.
</p>

<p>
Perhaps I find myself<br>
when I no longer need<br>
to fill every silence<br>
with the sound of my existence.
</p>

<p>
For what is fullness<br>
if not the fear of an empty space?<br>
And what is emptiness<br>
if not a space<br>
waiting to be understood?
</p>

<p>
The river does not keep its water.<br>
The sky does not hold its clouds.<br>
And still, the river flows,<br>
and still, the sky remains.
</p>

<p>
So let me remain<br>
a little empty,<br>
a little full,<br>
a little lost,<br>
a little found.
</p>

<p>
Let me be a question<br>
that does not hurry<br>
toward an answer.
</p>

<p>
For somewhere between<br>
everything I am<br>
and everything I am not,<br>
life continues to unfold.
</p>

<p>
And I, too,<br>
continue to become.
</p>

<p>
&mdash; Ujjal Timshina
</p>
