<h3><strong>The Art of Being Human</strong></h3>

<p>
Some mornings, I wake up curious about everything.<br>
I want to read, to learn, to wander,<br>
to understand things I have never understood before.<br>
The world seems full of possibilities,<br>
and I feel like there is so much life<br>
waiting to be discovered.
</p>

<p>
And then there are mornings<br>
when I would rather stay in bed a little longer.<br>
When even the simplest things seem complicated,<br>
when my thoughts refuse to settle,<br>
and I cannot quite explain<br>
what is happening inside my head.
</p>

<p>
I suppose both mornings belong to me.
</p>

<p>
Sometimes I understand something so deeply<br>
that I cannot wait to explore what comes next.<br>
I follow one thought into another,<br>
lose track of time,<br>
and forget that the rest of the world exists.
</p>

<p>
And sometimes I sit with the very same question<br>
for hours, feeling as though<br>
I have understood absolutely nothing.<br>
I read the same lines again,<br>
look at the same page again,<br>
and wonder how something so simple<br>
could make me feel so foolish.
</p>

<p>
Life has a funny way<br>
of reminding us how little we know.
</p>

<p>
There are moments when I want to leave everything,<br>
take a train towards the mountains,<br>
find a quiet trail through a deep forest,<br>
and walk without knowing where it leads.<br>
I imagine sitting beneath an old tree,<br>
listening to the leaves move with the wind,<br>
with no conversation to continue<br>
and no explanation to offer.
</p>

<p>
Not every silence needs to be filled.<br>
Not every thought needs to be followed.<br>
Not every question needs an answer.
</p>

<p>
Sometimes I wonder what I am doing,<br>
why I am doing it,<br>
and whether I have become so occupied<br>
with the things on my mind<br>
that I have forgotten to notice<br>
the life happening around me.
</p>

<p>
I wonder whether I am giving enough<br>
to the people who care about me.<br>
Whether I listen as much as I should.<br>
Whether I understand them<br>
or merely wait for my turn to speak.<br>
Whether I have been kind enough<br>
when someone needed a little kindness.
</p>

<p>
There are things I wish I had said,<br>
and things I wish I had left unsaid.<br>
There are conversations I replay<br>
long after they have ended,<br>
and people I miss without telling them.
</p>

<p>
Sometimes I laugh at the smallest things.<br>
A familiar tune, an unexpected message,<br>
a silly conversation that goes nowhere.<br>
Sometimes a cup of tea,<br>
a little sunlight entering the room,<br>
or the sound of rain outside<br>
is enough to make an ordinary day beautiful.
</p>

<p>
And sometimes, without any particular reason,<br>
I feel a little sad.<br>
Nothing terrible has happened.<br>
Nobody has said anything hurtful.<br>
Yet something feels distant,<br>
and I cannot find the right words<br>
to explain it, even to myself.
</p>

<p>
Perhaps being human means<br>
not always knowing what we feel,<br>
even when we are the ones feeling it.
</p>

<p>
We change our minds.<br>
We misunderstand people.<br>
We make assumptions and regret them.<br>
We get excited about things<br>
that we later find difficult.<br>
We sometimes want company<br>
and sometimes wish everyone would leave us alone.
</p>

<p>
We are full of contradictions,<br>
and perhaps that is perfectly ordinary.
</p>

<p>
I like the thought that we can begin again.<br>
We can open a book whose first page<br>
we do not understand.<br>
We can learn to play an instrument<br>
without knowing a single note.<br>
We can visit a place where nobody knows us,<br>
learn a language slowly,<br>
or become interested in something<br>
we had never imagined enjoying.
</p>

<p>
There is no particular age<br>
at which curiosity must come to an end.<br>
There is no rule that says<br>
we must remain the same person<br>
we were yesterday.
</p>

<p>
I hope I never lose that freedom.<br>
The freedom to be confused,<br>
to ask questions,<br>
to change direction,<br>
to admit that I was wrong,<br>
and to find something interesting<br>
in the most unexpected places.
</p>

<p>
I want to have days<br>
when I accomplish absolutely nothing<br>
and still feel that the day was worth living.<br>
Days spent walking without a destination,<br>
sitting with someone without much to say,<br>
or watching the evening slowly disappear.
</p>

<p>
I want to stop treating every moment<br>
as though it must lead somewhere.
</p>

<p>
Perhaps life is not always asking us<br>
to become something.<br>
Perhaps sometimes it is simply inviting us<br>
to notice what is already here.
</p>

<p>
The people around us,<br>
the places we pass every day,<br>
the things we keep postponing,<br>
the questions we have not yet asked,<br>
and the little joys we almost overlook.
</p>

<p>
I do not know how to live perfectly.<br>
I do not think I ever will.<br>
I will probably overthink tomorrow,<br>
forget something important,<br>
misunderstand something obvious,<br>
and spend an unreasonable amount of time<br>
trying to figure out a simple thing.
</p>

<p>
But I hope I continue to find reasons<br>
to be curious about another morning.
</p>

<p>
To learn something new.<br>
To forgive myself for something old.<br>
To sit quietly when the world gets too loud.<br>
To enjoy the company of people<br>
without wondering what comes next.
</p>

<p>
And perhaps, somewhere along the way,<br>
to become a little more comfortable<br>
with being exactly as unfinished<br>
as every other human being.
</p>

<p>
Maybe that is the art of being human:<br>
to keep discovering life<br>
without expecting to understand it completely,<br>
to make room for all that we feel,<br>
and to remain open<br>
to all that we have yet to experience.
</p>

<p>
After all, there is still so much<br>
we have not seen,<br>
so much we have not learned,<br>
and so much of ourselves<br>
we have not yet met.
</p>

<p>
&mdash; Ujjal Timshina
</p>
