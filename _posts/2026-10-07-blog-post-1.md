<h3><strong>The Corner That Grows Older With Me</strong></h3>
...

<p>
There is a corner<br>
in the place where I work,<br>
and it is growing older<br>
alongside me.
</p>

<p>
It is not a grand place.<br>
No one writes about it,
maybe!!<br>
  <br>

</p>

<p>
But every day,<br>
the pigeons come.<br>
They rise together,<br>
turn together,<br>
fold the sky into shapes<br>
I cannot name.
</p>

<p>
A wonderful pattern,<br>
born without rehearsal,<br>
without a leader,<br>
without a reason<br>
I can understand.
</p>

<p>
And I stand there<br>
and wonder—
</p>

<p>
what if we humans<br>
also flew together?
</p>

<p>
Not probably<br>
with an intention<br>
to form a pattern,<br>
but with a heart<br>
to fly.
</p>

<p>
Though it is very necessary<br>
not to fly behind<br>
and forward each other,<br>
but at least<br>
together.
</p>

<p>
This corner<br>
has seen me<br>
in many weathers.
</p>

<p>
Sometimes<br>
with bad emotions<br>
sitting heavy<br>
on my chest.
</p>

<p>
Sometimes<br>
with a bit of internal happiness<br>
so quiet<br>
I almost miss it.
</p>

<p>
Sometimes<br>
with stress<br>
wrapped around my shoulders<br>
like a coat<br>
I never asked for.
</p>

<p>
And sometimes<br>
with Bipul Chettri<br>
in my earphones,<br>
loud enough<br>
to drown the world,<br>
while my mind stays<br>
empty,
</p>

<p>
or as empty<br>
as a mind like mine<br>
can ever be.
</p>

<p>
I have several<br>
such corners.
</p>

<p>
Not just this one.<br>
Many of them<br>
live in Sikkim—<br>
in the folds of hills,<br>
in the quiet of old roads,<br>
in places<br>
that carry<br>
a lot more memories<br>
and stories<br>
than they were ever asked to hold.
</p>

<p>
I feel super happy<br>
to visit them.
</p>

<p>
I smile<br>
because they literally<br>
carry an emotion to me—<br>
a feeling<br>
I cannot always explain,<br>
but one I always recognize.
</p>

<p>
Perhaps that is what<br>
a corner really is:
</p>

<p>
not a place,<br>
but a witness.
</p>

<p>
And mine,<br>
here by the pigeons<br>
and the fading light,<br>
is growing older<br>
with me—
</p>

<p>
quietly,<br>
faithfully,<br>
still hoping<br>
we all learn<br>
to fly together.
</p>

<p>
&mdash; Ujjal Timshina
