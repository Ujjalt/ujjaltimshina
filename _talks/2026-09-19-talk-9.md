<strong style="color:#e8f5e8;">Turning Geometry into Algebra</strong> was a mathematics outreach session conducted under the <strong style="color:#e8f5e8;">Raising A Mathematician (RAM) Foundation and IIIT Delhi</strong>.
We love the symmetries and patterns around us so much that we often forget to stop and think about why they are beautiful. But how do we express that beauty in a language precise enough for someone else to see and appreciate it in the same way?
The session was a small attempt to explore this question through mathematics. We played with simple symmetries and geometric patterns, looked for things that caught our attention, and tried to translate those observations into the language of algebra. It was less about learning a particular technique and more about experimenting, noticing patterns, asking questions, and discovering how algebra can give a precise language to something we first see geometrically.
