<strong style="color:#e8f5e8;">Turning Geometry into Algebra</strong> was a mathematics outreach session conducted under the <strong style="color:#e8f5e8;">Raising A Mathematician (RAM) Foundation</strong>. The session brought together geometry and algebra through a collection of problems and discussions designed to encourage students to explore mathematical ideas on their own.

The session focused on how a geometric configuration can often be described using algebra, and how an algebraic expression can reveal something about the geometry behind it. Students were encouraged to move between diagrams and equations, look for patterns, make observations, and use those observations to develop mathematical arguments.

The emphasis was not on applying a fixed set of formulas, but on understanding how different representations of the same mathematical idea can complement one another. Through the problems and discussions, the session aimed to encourage curiosity, careful reasoning, and a more connected view of mathematics.
