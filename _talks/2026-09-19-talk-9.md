  <strong style="color:#e8f5e8;">Turning Geometry into Algebra</strong>, a mathematics outreach session conducted under the 
  <strong style="color:#e8f5e8;">Raising A Mathematician (RAM) Foundation</strong>, a not-for-profit organisation registered under the Bombay Trust Act, 1950. 
  The foundation works across multiple dimensions of mathematics education, with a focus on fostering mathematical thinking and nurturing interest in mathematics among young learners.



  <strong style="color:#e8f5e8;">Overview:</strong> My session focused on the interplay between geometry and algebra, illustrating how geometric configurations can be translated into algebraic expressions and investigated using mathematical reasoning. Through carefully designed problems and discussions, students were encouraged to move between visual and symbolic representations and to appreciate the connections between different areas of mathematics.



  <strong style="color:#e8f5e8;">Highlights:</strong>
  <ul style="color:#e8f5e8;">
    <li>Exploring the fundamental connections between geometry and algebra</li>
    <li>Translating geometric configurations and observations into algebraic expressions</li>
    <li>Developing mathematical reasoning through visual and problem-based exploration</li>
    <li>Encouraging curiosity, creative thinking, and a deeper appreciation of mathematics</li>
  </ul>
