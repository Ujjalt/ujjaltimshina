  <strong style="color:#e8f5e8;">Career Aspects in Mathematics</strong>, delivered at <strong style="color:#e8f5e8;">Sikkim Government College, Rhenock</strong>, Rhenock, Sikkim, India, in 2024, under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Sikkim. The talk was an opportunity to interact with undergraduate students and share how mathematics can lead to many different academic and professional paths. Drawing from real-life examples and personal experiences, the discussion touched on opportunities in research and higher studies, as well as careers in data science, finance, teaching, and industry. We also spoke about preparing for competitive examinations, developing a deeper understanding of mathematics, and, above all, keeping the curiosity to learn alive beyond the classroom.
